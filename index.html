          FAQ · 39 answers

          Questions

          Prices, booking, design and the studio. Didn't find your question? Message me on Instagram.

          Prices and payment

          How much does a Gel-X set cost?

          Gel-X starts at 400 SEK for short length (S) and goes up to 1000 SEK for XXL. Gel polish on your natural nail is 300 SEK. Removal, prep and one gel colour are included. Nail design adds 50 to 500 SEK for the whole set, and upper forms instead of Gel-X add 100 SEK. You can count your exact price on the booking page.

          Do I have to pay in advance?

          No. There is no prepayment. You pay at the studio after the appointment.

          How can I pay?

          Swish, card or cash. Cards from Russian banks don't work.

          Can I leave a tip?

          Yes, of course, in any form.

          Do you have discounts?

          Bring a friend and you both get 30% off. Every 6th manicure is free: just tell me you're a member.

          Are you looking for models?

          Yes. Models get a discount, and I choose the design. Message me on Instagram.

          How much does a refill cost?

          It depends on your nails. Message me on Instagram and I'll tell you the price.

          How much is removal without a new set?

          100 SEK.

          Do you do a manicure without polish?

          Yes, 100 SEK. That's a manicure without polish and without removal.

          Is French included in the price?

          French counts as tier 1 design, 50 SEK for the whole set.

          Booking

          How far ahead should I book?

          Best 1 to 2 days ahead. Same-day or next-day slots happen, but rarely.

          How fast do you confirm a booking?

          Within a few hours.

          How do I reschedule?

          Message me on Instagram and send a screenshot of your confirmation e-mail. You can reschedule no later than 14 hours before the appointment.

          What if I don't show up?

          The first time I forgive it. The second time you get blocked.

          What are the opening hours?

          Monday to Friday 10:00 to 19:00, Saturday 12:00 to 18:00. Closed on Sundays. An earlier Saturday start is sometimes possible, message me on Instagram.

          Where is the studio?

          A home studio in Upplands Väsby, north of Stockholm. You get the exact address with your booking confirmation.

          Services

          How long does an appointment take?

          About 2.5 hours for gel polish on natural nails with removal and strengthening. Extensions and nail art take longer: a very detailed set (tier 5) can take 5 to 6 hours. The booking page shows the approximate time for your set.

          What is the difference between Gel-X and upper forms?

          Gel-X uses ready-made soft gel tips and is the default. Upper forms (dual forms) are built with gel, cost 100 SEK more and take about 40 minutes longer.

          Which nail shapes do you do?

          All of them. Claw shapes cost more.

          Can every nail have a different length or design?

          Yes.

          Do you fix broken nails?

          Yes. If my work breaks within a week and it's clearly my fault, the repair is free, no matter how many nails.

          Can you remove nails done by another nail tech?

          Yes, removal of someone else's work costs 50 SEK.

          Can I come with very short or bitten nails?

          Yes.

          Do you do nails for men?

          Yes.

          From what age can I book?

          From 13. For teens I don't do extra long nails.

          Design

          Can I bring my own design idea?

          Yes. Send a screenshot, a moodboard or just a mood at least 2 days before your appointment. The final price follows the design you actually get.

          Can you copy a design one to one?

          One to one, only a printer can. I get as close as I can, but hand-made nails always come out a little different.

          I have no idea what I want. Can you help?

          Yes, we'll choose together.

          Are charms included?

          Usually yes, they are part of the design tier.

          Can I change the design during the appointment?

          Yes, but the price and time are planned for your tier, so the new design has to be the same tier.

          What if I don't like the result?

          Tell me. I can remove it the same day, but you still pay half the price plus removal: I've spent my time and materials. Please remember that I'm a beginner, I say it everywhere.

          Hygiene and health

          How do you clean your tools?

          Ultrasonic cleaner, then a dry heat sterilizer. Nail files are sterilized too, by soaking.

          I have a nail or skin problem. Can I still come?

          Leave a comment about it when you book and I'll take a look. In the worst case I'll send you to a specialist.

          Can I remove gel or Gel-X at home?

          No, never.

          At the studio

          How do I get there?

          From Upplands Väsby station it's a bus ride or a 10-minute walk. Parking is free for 4 hours.

          Is there a lift?

          Yes.

          What is there during the appointment?

          Coffee, tea and soda. You can watch whatever you want, and there's a phone charger.

          Can I take a break during a long appointment?

          Yes, for a smoke and so on. Vapes without a strong smell and snus are fine.

          Are you trained?

          Yes, I took a course and have a certificate.
