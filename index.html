Hi, I'm Diana, the one behind @hordi_nails

I'm 21 and from Ukraine. I haven't been doing nails for that long, but I've always been the picky, creative one who redoes a detail until it's right. So I opened my own small studio.

I love the detailed sets: raised chrome, lace French, charms, a different design on every nail. Plain colour is welcome too, I just get happy when someone brings a weird idea.

I work from my home studio in Upplands Väsby, one client at a time. My cats and a dachshund live here, so tell me if you're allergic.

Not sure what you want? Send a screenshot or just a mood, and we'll figure it out together. I'd rather take an extra half hour than let you leave with nails I'm not happy with.

        Questions

        How much does a Gel-X set cost?

        Gel-X starts at 300 SEK for short length (S) and goes up to 900 SEK for XXL. Gel polish on your natural nail is 200 SEK. Removal, prep and one gel colour are included. Nail design adds 50 to 500 SEK for the whole set, and upper forms instead of Gel-X add 100 SEK. You can count your exact price on the booking page.

        How long does an appointment take?

        About 2.5 hours for gel polish on natural nails with removal and strengthening. Extensions and nail art take longer: a very detailed set (tier 5) can take 5 to 6 hours. The booking page shows the approximate time for your set.

        Do I have to pay in advance?

        No. There is no prepayment. You pay at the studio after the appointment.

        Where is the studio?

        A home studio in Upplands Väsby, north of Stockholm. You get the exact address with your booking confirmation.

        What are the opening hours?

        Monday to Friday 10:00 to 19:00, Saturday 12:00 to 18:00. Closed on Sundays. An earlier Saturday start is sometimes possible, message me on Instagram.

        What is the difference between Gel-X and upper forms?

        Gel-X uses ready-made soft gel tips and is the default. Upper forms (dual forms) are built with gel, cost 100 SEK more and take about 40 minutes longer.

        Can I bring my own design idea?

        Yes. Send a screenshot, a moodboard or just a mood at least 2 days before your appointment. The final price follows the design you actually get.

        Can you remove nails done by another nail tech?

        Yes, removal of someone else's work costs 50 SEK.
